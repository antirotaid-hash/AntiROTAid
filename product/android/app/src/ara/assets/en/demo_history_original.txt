Slide 1:
Why events happen
Core idea:
• An important event rarely has only one cause.
• Some causes erupt suddenly, while others build up slowly over many years.
Types of causes:
• Economic, political, social, and technological causes, often mixed together.

Slide 2:
How to verify a claim
What a historian weighs:
• Documents, figures, maps, and testimony from people who lived at the time.
When a claim is solid:
• When several sources that are independent of one another say the same thing.

Slide 3:
What changes and what remains
Rapid change:
• After a crisis, leadership and rules can change almost overnight.
Continuity:
• Language, customs, and people’s way of life usually endure much longer.
