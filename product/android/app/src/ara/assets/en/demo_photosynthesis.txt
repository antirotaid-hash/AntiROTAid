Slide 1:
Photosynthesis in brief
What happens:
• A plant captures energy from light and uses it to make food.
• Carbon dioxide and water are turned into sugar (glucose) and oxygen.
In short:
• Light becomes chemical energy stored in sugar.

Slide 2:
What it needs
Three things:
• Light — the energy source.
• Carbon dioxide — enters through tiny pores in the leaf.
• Water — travels from the roots through the stem to the leaves.

Slide 3:
Where it happens
Location:
• Mainly in leaves, inside green compartments within the cells.
The role of the green color:
• The green pigment absorbs red and blue light well.
• Green light is reflected, which is why leaves look green to us.
