Slide 1:
What an equation means
Simple idea:
• An equation is like a balance: the left side of the “=” sign has the same value as the right side.
• Solving it means finding the hidden number that keeps the balance level.
Example:
• If x + 5 = 12, the number we are looking for is 7.

Slide 2:
The balance rule
Principle:
• Whatever operation you perform on one side, perform the same operation on the other so the balance remains level.
Allowed moves:
1. Add the same number to both sides.
2. Subtract the same number from both sides.
3. Multiply or divide both sides by the same non-zero number.

Slide 3:
When parentheses appear
Order:
• First expand the parentheses.
• Then combine like terms.
• Finally isolate the unknown.
Remember:
• A minus sign before parentheses changes every sign inside them.
